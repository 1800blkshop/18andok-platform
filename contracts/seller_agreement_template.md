# Seller Agreement Template (Non-Custodial, Draft — For Counsel Review)

WARNING: Draft for counsel review. Do not use without legal sign-off.

## Parties
- 18&ok LLC ("Company")
- Seller ("Seller")

## Purpose
Seller engages Company to identify buyers and facilitate sale logistics. Company acts as an introducer/agent and does NOT take title or custody by default; any transfer of title will be directly between Seller and Buyer or through a licensed escrow/title company.

## Fees & Payment
- Company will charge a commission or fee for services. Payment timing, escrow, and payout schedules are defined in Exhibit A.
- Seller acknowledges potential holdbacks subject to dispute resolution, but Company will not automatically accept or hold sale proceeds in its operating account unless an explicit escrow arrangement is documented.

## Title Transfer & Warranty
- Seller warrants it has full authority to transfer title and will provide provenance and related documentation prior to closing.

## KYC & AML
- Seller agrees to provide required KYC and compliance information and to be screened for sanctions and AML checks prior to any transaction.

## Indemnity & Liability
- Seller indemnifies Company against claims related to Seller misrepresentations of title/provenance.
