# Buyer Agreement Template (Non-Custodial, Draft — For Counsel Review)

WARNING: This is a template for internal use and counsel review only. Do not use in production without legal review.

## Parties
- 18&ok LLC ("Company") — matchmaker and service provider
- Buyer ("Buyer") — purchaser introduced to a seller by Company

## Purpose
Company locates and introduces Buyers to Sellers and facilitates coordination, negotiation, and logistics. Company does NOT take title to assets or hold settlement funds by default; transfers and settlement occur directly between Buyer and Seller or via a licensed escrow provider.

## Scope of Services
- Company will source and present assets to Buyer per described specifications.
- Company will facilitate introductions and assist in documentation and coordination.

## Fees & Payment
- Buyer agrees to pay Company a service fee as set out in the related invoice/statement.
- Company will capture fees from Buyer to Company's account. Buyer authorizes Company to charge the stated fee only after explicit consent and execution of the related agreement.

## Title & Custody
- Buyer acknowledges that Company does NOT take title to assets. Title transfer occurs directly between Buyer and Seller or through escrow/title company as agreed in the transaction documents.

## Risk & Insurance
- Buyer is responsible for verifying title, provenance, and insurance arrangements as specified in the transaction documents.

## Compliance & KYC
- Buyer agrees to provide KYC information and to be screened prior to transaction completion for AML and sanctions compliance.

## Dispute, Refund & Cancellation
- Cancellation and refund policies tied to service fees are defined in Exhibit X. Disputes follow the procedure in Section Y.
