# Buyer Agreement Template (Draft — For Counsel Review)

WARNING: This is a template for internal use and counsel review only. Do not use in production without legal review.

## Parties
- 18&ok LLC ("Company") — registered in Louisiana
- Buyer ("Buyer") — purchaser of assets sourced, held, or sold by Company

## Purpose
Company locates, vets, and may hold assets (stocks, real property, rare goods) on behalf of Company and markets/resells those assets to Buyers. Company is engaged as a service provider; unless explicitly agreed, Buyer understands Company may hold title to assets in the Company name prior to resale.

## Scope of Services
- Company will source and present assets to Buyer per described specifications.
- Company will facilitate purchase, custody (if applicable), and transfer of title when sale completes.

## Fees & Payment
- Buyer agrees to pay Company a service fee or purchase price as set out in the related invoice/statement.
- Payment terms, deposits, escrow requirements, and release conditions must be attached as an Exhibit.

## Title & Custody
- Buyer acknowledges that Company may hold title to assets in Company name until closing and that Buyer has reviewed the title/provenance documents prior to consummation.

## Risk & Insurance
- Company will maintain insurance for assets while held but Buyer is encouraged to confirm coverage and may obtain additional insurance for transport or storage per the agreement.

## Representations & Warranties
- Company represents that it has taken commercially reasonable steps to verify provenance and title.
- Buyer represents that it has authority and funding to purchase the asset.

## Dispute, Refund & Cancellation
- Cancellation and refund policies are defined in Exhibit X. Disputes should follow the procedure set forth in Section Y.

## Compliance & KYC
- Buyer agrees to provide KYC information and to be screened for sanctions and AML checks prior to any transaction.

## Limitation of Liability
- Company limits liability to fees paid to Company for the specific transaction, except in cases of willful misconduct or fraud.

## Governing Law
- State of Louisiana (specify parish/county), with arbitration or courts as agreed.

## Signatures
- [Buyer representative]  
- [On behalf of 18&ok LLC]
